## Why

The A/A/B window (`<ns>/rows.md`, `<ns>` = `/Users/Vincent/Workspace/albatrion/schema-form-assemble-first/.seiri/tasks/assemble-first-432/`) used one fresh process per scene, dist and round. A causality check (`<ns>/causality/`, section "입증 표시 행의 인과 확인" of the result document) showed that one process per round under-estimates process-to-process spread: byte-identical A and A′ differ with p ≈ 0 in some rows, and the A-run spread across fresh processes is about 1.6–1.8 %. The new branch runs only in the `object-B0` scenes (once per mount), so only those rows can show its effect. This brief re-measures exactly those rows with many interleaved fresh processes and decides by the median of per-run minimums.
